Privacy Policy for GripTracker

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the GripTracker mobile application ("the App").

1. Data Collection and Storage

GripTracker is designed with privacy in mind. All workout data you enter, including sessions, sets, exercises, equipment, personal records, and any photos you attach to equipment, is stored locally on your device. We do not run any servers that collect or store this information. Your workout data is not transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads. This data collection is governed by Google's privacy policy. We recommend you review it here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Deletion

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete all your workout sessions and personal records at any time by going to the App's Settings screen and selecting "Clear all sessions and personal records." Uninstalling the App from your device will also remove all locally stored data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: true.orbital.creation@gmail.com
